Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what data the Ki app (“Ki”, “the app”) handles, why, and what choices you have. It also covers this website.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. Ki never asks for your name, email or a password.
  • Your paths, workout history, streak and settings are stored on your phone, not on our servers. We don’t keep a database of users.
  • Pain reports and comfort options never leave your phone. They are left out of device backups, backup files and usage data.
  • Ki never asks about health conditions or diagnoses.
  • Ki has no ads and doesn’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app sends a small set of anonymous usage counts so we can improve it. You can turn this off in Profile › Privacy.
  • You can export or delete your data inside the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Ki is published by Ekin Uzunyayla, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the data described here (“we”, “us”).

Contact: ekinnuzunyayla@gmail.com

2. Data that stays on your phone

The following data is created and stored only in the app’s storage on your phone. We cannot see it.

  • Your paths, the steps you have unlocked and your answers to the starting questions.
  • Your workout history: sessions, sets, and the reps, hold times and weights you log.
  • Your weekly goal, streak and streak freeze.
  • Your settings: reminder days and time, sound, units, and your setup (such as whether you have dumbbells or stairs).
  • Your comfort options (“Knee-friendly options”, “Do this off the floor”) and anything you report with “Something hurts”.

Pain and comfort. “Something hurts” never asks where it hurts or why, and Ki never asks about conditions, injuries or diagnoses. Pain reports and comfort options are kept in a separate store on your phone that is excluded from device backups, from the files you export and from usage data.

Device backups. Your paths, history, streak and settings may be included in your phone’s own backup (Android backup, or an iPhone or iCloud backup), which is governed by Google’s or Apple’s terms. That backup is how progress moves to a new phone. We do not sync it and cannot read it.

Files you export. “Export history as CSV” and “Save a backup file” create a file on your phone and open your phone’s share sheet. Where the file goes from there is your choice.

Reminders. Workout reminders and the free-trial reminder are local notifications scheduled on your phone. We do not use a push-notification service.

3. Purchases

Ki Pro subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details.

We use RevenueCat to check what you have bought. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created on your phone, your purchase and subscription history from the store, and basic device and app information (such as platform, app version, country and language).

4. Usage data and crash reports

To learn whether Ki works for the people using it, the app sends a short, fixed list of usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. These are the only events:

  • The app was opened.
  • The starting questions were finished.
  • A session was started, and whether it was the shorter version.
  • A session was finished: how many of its blocks were done, whether it was the shorter version, and how many sessions you have completed so far.
  • An easier option was used. The event never says why: “Too hard” and “Something hurts” look the same to us.
  • The Ki Pro screen was shown, and whether it opened by itself.
  • Ki Pro was bought, and which plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime).
  • A mobility flow was finished.

Each event arrives with a random ID created on your phone, the time, and basic technical details such as app version, operating system, device model and language. Like any internet request, it comes from your IP address.

Usage events never contain pain reports, comfort options, the exercises you did, your reps, or anything you type.

Your choice. You can turn usage events off at any time: Profile › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data. It is on by default.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a report is sent to Sentry with technical details of the error, device model, operating system version and app version. Crash reports contain no screenshots or screen recordings.

5. What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or password. The app never asks for them.
  • Health conditions, diagnoses or injuries, and your age, weight, height or body measurements.
  • Data from Apple Health or Health Connect. Ki does not connect to either.
  • Camera, microphone, photos or contacts access.
  • Precise location.
  • An advertising ID. Ki has no ads.

6. Health-related information

Ki is a fitness app, so what you log can say something about your physical activity. That is why it stays on your phone: we do not receive your workout history, pain reports or comfort options. The usage events in section 4 contain counts only.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use only the service providers below, each for the purpose described:

  • Apple and Google (app stores and payments)
  • RevenueCat (purchase checks)
  • PostHog (usage events)
  • Sentry (crash reports)
  • Supabase (runs the small server function that deletes your purchase record when you use “Delete my data”; it stores no data about you)
  • GitHub (hosts this website)

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision, or, under Turkish law, on your explicit consent where no other basis applies.

10. How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete the app or use “Delete my data”. Copies in your own device backup stay until your phone replaces them.
  • Purchase record at RevenueCat: until you use “Delete my data”.
  • Usage events and crash reports: only as long as we need them to understand trends and fix problems, within the retention limits of those providers.
  • Purchase records at the stores: Apple and Google keep transaction records as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request.

11. Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile › Your data › Delete my data. This removes everything Ki stores on that phone: your paths and unlocked steps, workout history, weekly goal and streak, settings, reminders and comfort options. It also asks our server to delete your purchase record at RevenueCat.

Deleting your data does not cancel a Ki Pro subscription. Manage that in your App Store or Google Play settings.

If you no longer have the app, the data on your phone was removed with it. To have your purchase record deleted as well, write to ekinnuzunyayla@gmail.com and tell us which store you used and roughly when you bought. We will do our best to find it and will answer within 30 days.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR): you can complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698): you have the rights listed in Article 11, including learning whether your data is processed, asking for correction or deletion, and learning which third parties receive it. You can apply to us by email and, if you are not satisfied, to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).
  • California and other US states: you have the right to know, delete and correct your data, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Canada, Australia and New Zealand: you can ask for access to and correction of your personal information, and complain to your privacy regulator.

Ki has no accounts, so we usually cannot tell which data is yours. Most of it is on your phone, where you can export or delete it yourself. If you write to us, we will tell you what we can do and reply within 30 days.

13. Children

Ki is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

14. Security

Data sent between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit. What Ki stores on your phone is protected by your phone’s own security, such as its screen lock. No method is perfectly secure, so we keep what leaves your phone to the minimum.

15. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

16. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
