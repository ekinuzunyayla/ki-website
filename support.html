Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email ekinnuzunyayla@gmail.com. It helps if you include your phone model and the app version. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, email or password, and Ki works offline.

What do I need to start?

No gym equipment. You use things you already have at home: a wall, a sturdy chair against the wall, the stairs if you have them, a backpack and a few water bottles. Dumbbells are optional, and dumbbell mode is free.

What is free, and what is Ki Pro?

The core program is free and has no ads: all four paths, the workout player with voice cues, automatic progression, the easier and pain-free options, the shorter session, the weekly streak and streak freeze, your history, and CSV and JSON export.

Ki Pro is optional. It adds a 10-minute mobility flow for rest days and advanced charts. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

A move is too hard, or something hurts. What do I do?

Tap Make it easier, which is on screen during every set. “Too hard” gives you an easier version for today. “Something hurts” offers a pain-free alternative, or lets you skip the move or end the session. Your progress is kept.

Stop if you feel sharp pain. If it continues, check with a professional.

What happens to my streak on rest days, or in a week I miss?

The streak is weekly. You choose a goal of 2, 3 or 4 sessions a week, and rest days never break it. A free streak freeze covers a week you miss, and a new one comes back after a few weeks. You can also pause a week from the Today screen.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your progress is stored on your phone, not on our servers. There are two ways to move it:

  • Your phone’s own backup (Android backup, or an iPhone or iCloud backup) carries it to a new phone of the same kind.
  • A backup file: go to Profile › Your data › Save a backup file, send the file to your new phone, then use Restore from a backup file there. This also works between iPhone and Android.

Comfort options are never part of a backup, so set them again on the new phone. If you delete the app without either backup, your progress is gone and we can’t bring it back.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap Restore purchases on the Ki Pro screen or in Profile, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. Ki Pro bought on iPhone doesn’t carry over to Android, or the other way round.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Ki.
  • Android: Google Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Ki.

You keep Ki Pro until the end of the period you paid for. The free trial is on the yearly plan only, and Ki reminds you before it ends. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription. For refunds, contact Apple or Google.

How do I turn off usage data?

Go to Profile › Privacy and turn off Share anonymous usage data. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent while it is on.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile › Your data › Delete my data. You can export your history as CSV first. Deleting your data doesn’t cancel a Ki Pro subscription. More in the Privacy Policy.

Is Ki right for me if I have a health condition?

Ki is not medical advice. If you have a known heart, metabolic or kidney condition, or symptoms like chest pain, dizziness or unexplained shortness of breath, talk to a doctor before you start. Ki isn’t designed for pregnancy or the early postpartum period.
